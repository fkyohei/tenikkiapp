Support (Tenikki)

Tenikki is a handwriting-only diary app. There is no keyboard input — because you can't type, your own handwriting of that day remains.

FAQ

Q. What does “Confirm” do?

When you finish a day's page, tap “Confirm” to make it read-only. Entries are never confirmed automatically at midnight, so you can finish yesterday's page today. You can unlock a confirmed entry at any time.

Q. My iPad entries don't appear on my iPhone

  • Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple account.
  • Check that iCloud is enabled for the App in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
  • Sync may take a little while to propagate.

Q. I don't receive notifications

  • Reminders arrive only on days you haven't written yet. No reminder is sent on days you already wrote.
  • Check that notifications are allowed for the App in iOS Settings → Notifications.

Q. Pro was lost after switching devices

Try “Restore Purchase” in the App's settings. You need to be signed in with the same Apple account you used for the purchase.

Q. I want to type with a keyboard

The App has no keyboard input. This is not a bug — it is the whole concept of the App: writing by hand only.
